Emissions and DEF repair.

Modern diesel trucks rely on emissions systems to run right. When something goes wrong, your truck can light up the dash or even cut its own power. We diagnose and repair emissions and DEF problems in our Sparks shop.

What DEF does

Diesel exhaust fluid (DEF) is sprayed into the exhaust of most newer diesel trucks to cut harmful emissions. If the DEF system or another emissions part fails, the truck may warn you and then limit its power until it's fixed.

Fixed right the first time

Emissions codes can point to several possible parts. We track down the real cause, so you only pay for the repair you actually need, and we talk with you before any work is booked.

Signs it's time to call

  • A DEF or emissions warning light
  • Your truck limiting its power or speed
  • A message to refill or service the DEF system
  • Worse fuel mileage than usual
