Diesel repair · Sparks & Reno

Diesel truck repair in Sparks.

Dynamic Diesel has been fixing diesel trucks in Sparks since 2006. Light, medium and heavy duty, any make, one truck or a whole fleet. If it's mechanical, we fix it.

We work on gas trucks too, so if you have both in the driveway or in your fleet, one shop can handle them.

What we fix

  • Engine diagnostics, repair and leak repair, up to full engine replacement
  • Transmission repair and replacement
  • Brakes, front end, steering and suspension
  • Alignments
  • Emissions and diesel exhaust fluid (DEF) systems
  • A service, B service and DOT inspections

We find the real cause first

Diesel trucks throw codes for all kinds of reasons, and the code isn't always the whole story. We diagnose the actual problem before we start replacing parts, and we talk with you about what we found before any work is booked.

Mechanical only

We don't do body work. All of our focus goes into getting your truck running right.

Signs it's time to call

  • Strange noises or vibrations
  • Warning lights on your dash
  • Low power
  • Fluid leaks
