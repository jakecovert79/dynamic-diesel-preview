About Dynamic Diesel

Fixing trucks since 2006.

Two decades later, we're still the local shop people in Sparks and Reno call when their truck needs real mechanical work.

Who we are

Your one stop shop.

Dynamic Diesel is a mechanical repair shop at 2080 E Greg St in Sparks. About half of what we do is for everyday drivers with a truck that needs fixing. The other half is for companies and fleets, including police, fire and ambulance vehicles.

We work on diesel and gas trucks, light to heavy duty, any make. If it's mechanical, we fix it. We don't do body work, so all of our focus goes into getting your truck running right.
