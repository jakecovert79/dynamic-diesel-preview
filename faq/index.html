FAQ

Good questions.

Quick answers to what people ask us most. Don't see yours? Call us at 775-359-4477.

Do you do body work?

No. We're a mechanical shop. If it's mechanical, we fix it, but we don't do body work.

Do you work on gas trucks?

Yes. We work on diesel and gas trucks, any make, not just domestic.

What size trucks do you work on?

Light, medium and heavy duty trucks, plus service trucks.

What's the difference between A service and B service?

Think of A service as a routine checkup: an oil change and a safety inspection. B service is the deeper exam, a more in-depth maintenance visit with extra checks, done less often. Call us and we'll help you pick.

Does an oil change include anything else?

Yes. Every oil change includes a full safety inspection, so small problems get caught before they turn into expensive repairs.

Do I need an appointment?

We like to talk with you before any work is booked, so give us a call at 775-359-4477 or request a call back, and we'll find a time that works.

Do you work on company fleets and first responder vehicles?

Yes. About half of our work is for companies and fleets, including service trucks and police, fire and ambulance vehicles.

Can I wait while you work on my truck?

You're welcome to. Our waiting room has reclining chairs, a big TV and internet, so you can relax or get some work done.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 8:00 am to 5:00 pm.

Where are you located?

2080 E Greg St, Sparks, NV 89431. We serve drivers and fleets across Sparks and Reno.

We'd rather talk with you before any work is booked. Give us a call, or ask us to call you back.
